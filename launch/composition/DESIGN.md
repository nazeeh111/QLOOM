# QLOOM film design

## Style Prompt
A precise scientific instrument on warm paper, with a woven quantum path emblem. Space and readable evidence carry the presentation; blue circuit paths and amber physical transformations create the visual accent. The working Studio and genuine demo data are the centerpiece.

## Colors
- #f3f2ec: warm paper
- #203239: ink
- #315d7a: computational path
- #bc7e35: selected route
- #fffefa: Studio surface

## Typography
System sans for statements, system monospace for mappings and evidence. 76–106px headlines, 26px body, 20px labels. No remote fonts.

## What NOT to Do
No generic gradients, floating particles, fictional quantum hardware, benchmark superiority claim, fake progress, or unlabeled simulated results. No narration.
