# Brag Plan: QLOOM

## Inspection rubric
1. QLOOM learns which of 24 native compilation configurations are worth evaluating, then verifies every evaluated implementation.
2. The meaningful claim is “Explore fewer strategies. Check every result.” It makes no universal superiority claim.
3. The visual hook is real circuit wires becoming a physical route, with the warm ivory, ink, blue and amber Studio identity.
4. Show the actual Studio screenshot and report-derived processor mapping, not a fictional dashboard.
5. Twenty seconds carries a hook, product view, evidence, and a repository address.
6. Polished: a precise laboratory instrument, quietly confident and legible.
7. An original locally synthesized ambient score and three restrained original plucks. No narration. Bundled music has no verified redistribution license in its README, so none is used.
8. QLOOM: learned, budgeted quantum compilation with explicit mappings, independently verified semantics, and a local CPU Studio.
9. Import a circuit and choose hardware → compile and verify → inspect physical routing and export the evidence.

## The angle
The computation is the fixed thread. The physical route can change. Begin with a real circuit excerpt, reveal the working Studio, and end on independent semantics evidence and the repository. This is an authentic launch of a bounded research system; no claim of general compiler superiority.

## Identity
Canvas #f3f2ec, ink #203239, blue #315d7a, amber #bc7e35. System sans statements, system mono technical evidence. Original woven mark. No borrowed stock assets.

## Storyboard / 20 seconds / 1920×1080
- 0–4 s: “Same computation. A different route.” QLOOM wordmark and an actual input-circuit excerpt from the retained demo, labeled as an excerpt. Warm canvas; line work carries the topic.
- 4–10 s: “Compile. Verify. Inspect.” Actual Studio screenshot in a precisely framed panel, with “Learned, budgeted search” and “Local CPU · simulated hardware”. Soft crossfade handoff; no invented loading or progress.
- 10–16 s: “Every candidate independently verified.” Report-derived topology, selected candidate’s recorded mapping, hard budget of 4 evaluated configurations out of 24, and actual independent maximum residual. Phase and mapping checks are explicit. These numbers are one recorded demo, not a benchmark superiority claim.
- 16–20 s: QLOOM mark and “Your circuit. Every decision inspectable.” GitHub address. Slow settled hold. No noise-quality claim, no fake QPU footage.
